# AI Jarvis Grandmaster Creation Studios

## Product concept

A public AI Shop and hands-on AI Workshop inside **aievolutionaryevolutions.com**. Visitors discover practical AI, Linux, programming, blockchain, and business-building products; complete free challenges; book workshops; buy digital resources; and optionally apply to the affiliate program.

AI Jarvis coordinates approved content and evidence behind the scenes. It is not presented as an autonomous employee, financial adviser, guaranteed-income system, or unrestricted agent.

## Supported first release on Hostinger

Use Hostinger Ecommerce for:

- digital downloads;
- low-price guides and toolkits;
- workshop or consultation appointments;
- gift cards or donations if later approved;
- a single product catalog shared with the Hostinger storefront.

Do not represent native subscriptions, a multi-vendor marketplace, or a complete course platform as supported. Membership subscriptions require a separately reviewed third-party integration.

## Site structure

1. **AI Shop** — curated products with clear outcomes, audience, contents, price, support, license, refund terms, and disclosures.
2. **AI Workshop** — free beginner challenge, live/recorded workshop listings, build-room schedule, learning tracks, and project demonstrations.
3. **Grandmaster Creation Studios** — explain the nine studios, current prototypes, verified milestones, and safety gates.
4. **AI Jarvis Assistant** — capabilities, evidence, limitations, roadmap, and public repositories.
5. **Affiliate Program** — eligibility, approved assets, tracked links, commission rules, prohibited claims, disclosures, payment terms, and audit process.
6. **Learning Library** — free samples, tutorials, documentation, and product updates.
7. **Projects** — Prompt #11, Repository Doctor, Programming Powerhouse, Hermes Fabric, MemPalace, and approved future projects.
8. **Trust Center** — privacy, security, AI disclosure, provenance, accessibility, terms, refund policy, affiliate disclosure, and contact.

## Customer journey

Free 5-Day Challenge → $1 Field Guide → $2 Affiliate Edition → $7 Evidence & Product Ledger → workshop appointment → optional membership when its payment and access integration is approved.

Each step must deliver standalone value. Do not require an affiliate purchase, income claim, or recruitment to access a purchased product.

## System boundaries

- Hostinger: public pages, store catalog, checkout, supported appointment booking, hosting.
- AI Jarvis: drafts, catalog metadata, QA, provenance, product readiness, and safe workflow coordination.
- GitHub: versioned public-safe content and implementation evidence.
- CRM: consented lead/customer status and support history.
- Stripe or another approved gateway: payments or subscription billing where supported.
- Affiliate system: link attribution and commission ledger only after legal, tax, payment, and disclosure rules are approved.

## Launch gates

- Product files completed and malware scanned.
- Prices and license terms approved.
- Refund, privacy, affiliate, AI-use, and earnings disclaimers reviewed.
- Checkout tested using permitted test mode.
- Mobile and accessibility review passed.
- Analytics and consent configuration verified.
- No secret keys in pages, repositories, prompts, or client-side code.
- Charles approves publication, payment activation, and affiliate enrollment.
