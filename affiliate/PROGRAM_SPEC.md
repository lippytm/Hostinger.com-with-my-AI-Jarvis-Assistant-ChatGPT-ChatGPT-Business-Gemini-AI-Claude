# AI Jarvis Grandmaster Creation Studios Affiliate Program

Status: design draft; enrollment and commissions are not active.

## Purpose

Allow approved affiliates to introduce customers to genuine AI Shop products and workshops using accurate, disclosed, trackable promotions.

## Required controls before launch

- written affiliate agreement;
- eligibility and termination rules;
- approved commission amount and attribution window;
- refund and chargeback handling;
- tax and payment information collection through an appropriate secure service;
- affiliate-link tracking and reconciliation;
- privacy and cookie consent review;
- clear affiliate and material-connection disclosure;
- prohibited-claims policy;
- product and brand asset approval;
- support and dispute process;
- owner approval.

## Prohibited claims

Affiliates may not claim:

- guaranteed earnings, wealth, employment, financing, or business success;
- that AI Jarvis is conscious, infallible, autonomous, or capable of unrestricted action;
- that a prototype is production-ready, secure, connected, or self-healing without published evidence;
- official partnership or endorsement by OpenAI, GitHub, Hostinger, Anthropic, Google, or another provider unless documented;
- false scarcity, fabricated testimonials, or misleading discounts.

## Proposed first pilot

- Product: Prompt #11 Affiliate Edition.
- Participants: maximum five manually approved pilot affiliates.
- Duration: 30 days after all controls are approved.
- Measurement: unique visits, consented leads, completed purchases, refunds, support load, accurate disclosures, and net revenue.
- Stop conditions: tracking mismatch, misleading promotion, privacy issue, chargeback spike, security concern, or negative unit economics.

## Data minimization

Affiliates receive only their link identifier, approved public assets, policy documents, and summarized commission statements. They do not receive customer payment data, credentials, private repositories, internal prompts, or unnecessary personal information.
